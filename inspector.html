Inspector Field Guide & FAQs

Procedural instructions for standard package inspections and Legal Metrology Rule 2011 compliance.

1 How do I capture a compliant, clear label photo for AI scanning? ▼

To ensure high AI OCR accuracy (above 95%):

  • Capture the Principal Display Panel (PDP) directly front-facing, avoiding glare and reflections.
  • Ensure all mandatory text (MRP, Net Quantity, Mfg Date, and Customer Helpline) is clearly within frame.
  • Hold the device steady in adequate lighting. Blurry or skewed photos can cause false OCR rejections.
2 What are the 6 mandatory declarations required under Rule 6? ▼

Under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, every pre-packaged commodity must display:

  1. Generic / Commodity Name: Clear commercial description.
  2. Net Quantity: In standard metric units (g, kg, ml, L, or piece count).
  3. Retail Sale Price (MRP): Inclusive of all taxes with currency symbol (₹ or Rs.).
  4. Manufacturer / Packer / Importer Details: Complete registered name and geographic address.
  5. Month & Year: Manufacturing, packaging, or import date.
  6. Consumer Care Details: Contact number, email address, or grievance redressal address.
3 What is the difference between 'Save as Draft' and 'Submit for Review'? ▼

Save as Draft: Keeps the inspection saved on your local device. You can re-open it, upload another photo, or edit extracted details anytime. It does not appear in the legal docket.

Submit for Review: Transmits the case directly to the Metrology Officer's Review Docket for legal scrutiny, notice issuance, and final approval/rejection under the Act.

4 How are weight tolerances (Maximum Permissible Errors) calculated? ▼

Tolerances (Maximum Allowable Variation - MAV) follow the Second Schedule of the Rules:

  • Packages up to 500g: Typically ± 2% or 9g.
  • Packages between 1kg and 5kg: ± 1.5% maximum allowable variation.
  • Packages exceeding 5kg: ± 1.0% maximum allowable variation.
  • Important Rule: The average net quantity of a sample batch must equal or exceed the declared net quantity, and no individual package may deviate by more than twice the MAV.
5 What should I do if the AI OCR misidentifies a date, price, or font size? ▼

The AI OCR acts as an assistive inspection tool. If optical recognition misreads an unusual stylized typeface:

  • You can save the scan as a Draft and capture another photo under better lighting.
  • Submit the case with your field remarks; the Metrology Officer has full judicial override capabilities in the Case Evidence workspace.
  • If the manufacturer used a font below the statutory minimum height (per Rule 7), this constitutes a defect and should be flagged as an 'Incorrect Font Size' violation.
