Section 36: Penalty for Selling, etc., of Non-Standard Packages

Act of 2009

(1) Whoever manufactures, packs, imports, sells, distributes, delivers or offers or exposes for sale, or has in his possession for sale, any pre-packaged commodity which does not conform to the declarations on the package as required under the rules, shall be punished with fine which may extend to twenty-five thousand rupees, for the second offence, to fifty thousand rupees and for the subsequent offence, with fine which shall not be less than fifty thousand rupees but which may extend to one lakh rupees or with imprisonment for a term which may extend to one year or with both.

Rule 6: Mandatory Declarations to be Made on Every Package

PCR 2011

Every package shall bear thereon or on a label securely affixed thereto, the following statutory declarations:

  • (a) The name and complete registered address of the manufacturer, or where manufacturer is not packer, name and address of manufacturer and packer;
  • (b) The generic or commodity name of the pre-packaged commodity contained in the package;
  • (c) The net quantity, in terms of standard unit of weight or measure, of the commodity;
  • (d) The month and year in which the commodity is manufactured, packed or imported;
  • (e) The maximum retail sale price (MRP) inclusive of all taxes;
  • (f) The name, address, telephone number, and email address of the person or office which may be contacted in case of consumer complaints.

Rule 7: Principal Display Panel & Minimum Font Height

PCR 2011

The height of any numeral and letter in the declaration shall not be less than the minimum height specified in the table based on the area of the principal display panel. For packages exceeding 500 sq cm, numeral height must not be less than 4.0 mm to guarantee prominent consumer legibility.

Rule 9: Manner of Declaration of Retail Sale Price (MRP)

PCR 2011

The retail sale price shall clearly include the currency symbol ₹ or words 'Rs.' along with the maximum retail price inclusive of all taxes. Dual pricing and overwriting of declared MRP on packaged goods is strictly prohibited under Rule 18(2).

Rule 32: Power to Inspect, Seize Samples, and Issue Compounding Orders

Enforcement

Any Legal Metrology Officer may inspect packages at any premises, seize non-conforming specimens as evidence, and initiate compounding under Section 48 upon payment of statutory compounding fees by the manufacturer or packer.
