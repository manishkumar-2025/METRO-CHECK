Comprehensive Statutory Inspection & Enforcement Ecosystem

The e-Legal Metrology Compliance & Enforcement Platform (e-LMCEP / METRO-CHECK) is India’s enterprise digital solution built for field inspectors, zonal officers, and consumer protection controllers. It digitizes enforcement of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Legal Metrology (Pre-Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (PCR 2011) across all 6 national zonal jurisdictions.

Mandatory Rule 6 Statutory Declarations

Every pre-packaged commodity sold in India must display these 6 mandatory declarations legibly under Rule 6(1) of the Legal Metrology (Pre-Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

Rule 6(1)(a)

Manufacturer / Packer / Importer Details

Name and complete legal address of the manufacturer, packer, or importer. Missing address or vague P.O. Box numbers constitute a non-compoundable statutory breach under Section 36.

Rule 6(1)(aa)

Country of Origin

Explicit declaration of the country of origin for imported pre-packaged commodities. Mandatory for transparency under foreign trade & consumer welfare mandates.

Rule 6(1)(b)

Common or Generic Commodity Name

The generic or common trade name of the commodity contained within the package to prevent misleading brand terminology or consumer deception.

Rule 6(1)(c)

Net Quantity & Standard Units

Net weight or volume expressed in standard SI units (g, kg, ml, L, N). Any shortfall exceeding Schedule II Maximum Allowable Variation (MAV) triggers short-quantity seizure under Section 36.

Rule 6(1)(d)

Date of Manufacture / Packing / Import

Month and year of manufacture or pre-packing. Crucial for perishable commodities and shelf-life verification under consumer safety guidelines.

Rule 6(1)(e) & (f)

MRP (Incl. of All Taxes) & Helpline

Maximum Retail Price in mandatory format: MRP ₹ xx.xx (incl. of all taxes) alongside a functioning consumer care telephone number and email address.

System Capabilities

6 Core Operational Modules

Module 01

Gemini AI Optical Vision Capture

Integrates Google Gemini AI Vision Engine for real-time extraction of curved, reflective, or distorted label text on field inspections. Extracts all mandatory Rule 6 attributes in real time.

  • ✓ Auto-rectification of packaging glare
  • ✓ Dual-photo front & back verification
Module 02

Automated Infraction Engine

Rules-based compounding calculator cross-checks net quantity declared against physical weight readings. Automatically determines Maximum Allowable Variation (MAV) tolerances under Schedule II.

  • ✓ Section 18 declaration breach logic
  • ✓ Section 36 short-weight compounding
Module 03

Zonal Adjudication Portal

Provides Metrology Officers (LMOs) with a 3-pane adjudication console to review field photos, verify legal violation formulas, approve compounding fees, or escalate repeat offenses to judicial court.

  • ✓ 3-Pane responsive mobile/tablet layout
  • ✓ 1-Click Form-V compounding approval
Module 04

Statutory Form-V PDF Generator

Generates official, court-admissible PDF seizure receipts, inspection reports, and compounding notices complete with digital signature placeholders, QR verification hashes, and offline print fallbacks.

  • ✓ Native PDF engine with jsPDF fallback
  • ✓ Cryptographic verification QR embedding
Module 05

Pan-India Master Audit Ledger

Real-time zonal dashboard for central controllers. Tracks inspections across North, South, East, West, Central, and North East zones, flagging non-compliant manufacturers and repeat offenders.

  • ✓ 6 Zonal jurisdiction metrics
  • ✓ Horizontal tablet scroll protection
Module 06

Offline Sync & Quota Protection

Built for remote field operations with spotty connectivity. Features automatic Local Storage quota management, client-side canvas image compression, and central background synchronization.

  • ✓ Storage quota overflow protection
  • ✓ Offline queueing with background sync

Enforcement Operations

6-Step Field-to-Court Workflow Lifecycle

STEP 01 → Inspector Portal
Optical Capture

Field Inspector captures commodity label photo via mobile or desktop camera interface.

STEP 02 → Gemini Vision
AI OCR Extraction

Gemini AI Vision Engine extracts mandatory declarations, MRP, Net Weight, and Manufacturer info.

STEP 03 → Rules Engine
Violation Assessment

System compares extracted text against Rule 6 requirements and MAV Schedule II limits.

STEP 04 → Inspector Signoff
Report Submission

Inspector verifies extracted fields, attaches physical scale reading, and submits inspection docket.

STEP 05 → Officer Console
Judicial Adjudication

Metrology Officer evaluates offense compounding, signs Form-V notice, or forwards to judicial magistrate.

STEP 06 Central Master Ledger
Audit & PDF Docket

Case syncs to central Master Ledger, issuing tamper-proof PDF seizure memos with QR hashes.

Enterprise Security Standard

Security, Privacy & Sovereign Integrity

🛡️ Sovereign Data Protection

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Strict isolation between Field Inspector, Legal Metrology Officer (LMO), and Zonal Admin roles prevents unauthorized notice generation or record tampering.

Local Storage Quota Defense

Automatic client-side FIFO cache pruner safeguards against browser Local Storage quota overflow crashes during heavy offline field inspections.

Cryptographic Hash Verification

Every generated Form-V PDF seizure notice includes embedded SHA-256 hash strings and dynamic QR codes for court verification.
