e-Legal Metrology Compliance & Enforcement Portal

METRO-CHECK National Digital Inspection System

A centralized statutory verification platform built for India's Legal Metrology Division:

  • • Standardizes optical label inspection & penalty compounding.
  • • Enforces PCR 2011 rules across all 6 Zonal Jurisdictions.
  • • Multi-tier adjudication from Field Inspector to National Admin.

National Compliance & Enforcement Mandate

Empowering Metrology Inspectors and Officers to audit mandatory declarations (MRP, Net Weight, Mfg Date, Country of Origin, Customer Care) and issue Form-V seizure receipts in real time.
